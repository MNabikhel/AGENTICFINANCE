Aether · sim:aether-1 · no live money

A rulebook for software that spends money.

A human writes a permission slip. Agents go shopping. A referee that never guesses says yes, no, or ask a grown-up. A notary writes it down. An auditor may read the book — and may not spend.

Two stories. The shopping trip is a human in the loop. Night watch is standing permission — L5 — with a handshake, a daily fuse, and a freeze that actually works.

The story appears here after you run it. Nothing is connected to a bank. These are simulated dollars, on purpose.

How to think about it

  • Permission slip = mandate (what the human allowed).
  • Referee = policy kernel (yes / no / ask a grown-up). It does not use an AI to “feel” risk.
  • Escrow = money locked until the work is done.
  • Notary = hash-chained audit log. Rewrite yesterday and it breaks.
  • Ladder L0→L5 = how far the human steps back. Freeze still works at the top.
